## Important movement-data limits

Several historically important movement studies did not release their original coordinate tracks:
- first-instar *B. germanica* bounded-locomotion tracks;
- *L. bostrychophila* movement-assay trajectories;
- Mowery *O. surinamensis* EthoVision XY coordinates;
- *C. cancroides* detailed JEB Tables S1/S2 and high-speed publisher media are not treated as redistributable unless reuse permission is clear.

R8.3 therefore bundles every useful open raw behavior/study dataset it can legally acquire, plus machine-readable published measurements for those non-released studies. Do not fabricate missing coordinates from graphs or summary statistics.

## Chelifer predation boundary

The direct predation experiment used *Varroa destructor* and *Liposcelis entomophila*. Transfer to rendered *L. bostrychophila* is same-genus evidence. Broader references include ants as prey, but there is no *Linepithema humile*-specific kill-rate/pursuit/preference calibration.

Optional ecology can model an evidence-classified close-range interaction. Strict Realistic must not invent a homing predator system.
