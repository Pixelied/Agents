# R8.3 empirical acquisition targets

This file documents the **intended** empirical sources. The final release manifest records what actually downloaded. A target listed here is not counted as bundled unless it has a local path, byte size and SHA-256 in `EMPIRICAL_DATA_MANIFEST.csv`.

## Blattella germanica

Priority behavior / movement:
- PMC9385682 — male/female/nymph 15-min shelter preference + 24-h adult shelter preference XLSX tables.
- PMC6393502 — original Dataset 1 XLSX with locomotor/grooming/courtship observations + open supplementary behavior video.
- PMC6804909 — original courtship locomotor/posture supplementary videos where the OA package permits redistribution.
- Dryad 10.5061/dryad.6q573n5zz — behavioral olfactory-learning data.
- Dryad 10.5061/dryad.t76hdr81k — raw courtship-event and life-history data.

Other useful raw exact-species data:
- Dryad 10.5061/dryad.063h8 — intake, growth, body mass, nitrogen, survival.
- Dryad 10.5061/dryad.6tt66 — behavioral/electrophysiological taste-response tables.
- Dryad 10.5061/dryad.jsxksn08r — feeding acceptance/consumption and sugar-aversion data.
- Dryad 10.5061/dryad.f82ns — nutrient consumption / maturation data.

The exact ~24 h first-instar Jeanson movement study remains a published-measurement calibration because its original tracks were not released.

## Liposcelis bostrychophila

- Mendeley Data 10.17632/5zdf96kr72.1 — CC BY 4.0 XLSX with acute/sublethal effects, reproduction and hatchability.
- PMC12028104 — supplementary fitness/Rickettsia data.
- PMC11737068 — developmental-stage/olfactory tables.
- PMC3835895 — transcriptome/validated-expression tables (small contextual data only).
- Published movement aggregate: Guedes et al. DOI 10.1002/ps.1634.
- Published life-history aggregate: Wang et al. 2000 DOI 10.1603/0013-8746(2000)093[0261:DAROTP]2.0.CO;2.

The original Guedes movement trajectories were not found in an open repository and remain an explicit raw-data gap.

## Oryzaephilus surinamensis

- Zenodo 4939311 / Dryad 10.5061/dryad.80dk1 — 7 source XLSX files: cuticle thickness/melanization, CHCs, water loss, mortality, population growth, qPCR.
- Edmond 10.17617/3.5l — raw quantitative cuticle measurements.
- Edmond 10.17617/3.5s — age-series cuticle/predation/fungal-defense data.
- PMC4641671 — individual knockdown and lethality-index XLSX datasets.
- PMC8113238 — source XLSX for cuticle/symbiont work.
- PMC13043703 — 5.6 MB source-data XLSX for survival/fitness/symbiont replacement.
- Published movement aggregates:
  - Mowery et al. 2004 DOI 10.1603/0046-225X-33.1.75;
  - Kavallieratos et al. 2024 DOI 10.1002/ps.8262, full Table 3 mobility treatment matrix.

Original Mowery EthoVision XY tracks were not publicly archived.

## Chelifer cancroides

- PMC8778599 — raw aphid/cytotoxic venom-assay supplementary archive.
- Published/direct locomotion values from Tross et al. 2022 DOI 10.1242/jeb.243930.
- Publisher high-speed movie and Tables S1/S2 are remotely indexed but are not redistributed unless reuse rights are clear.
- 2026 predation study remains the direct source for *Varroa destructor* / *Liposcelis entomophila* prey evidence.

No *Linepithema humile*-specific kill-rate or pursuit dataset is claimed.
