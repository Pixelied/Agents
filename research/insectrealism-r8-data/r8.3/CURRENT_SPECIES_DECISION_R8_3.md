# InsectRealism R8.3 — current species decision

R8.3 does **not** change the R8.2 species selection. It changes the quality and balance of the research evidence supplied to implementation.

## Core species — preserve full existing system

**Linepithema humile — Argentine ant**

Preserve the existing ant system, including realistic mature-worker sizing and continuous morphology/size variation, callows, queens, males, eggs, larvae, pupae/brood, corrected worker-vs-male larval evidence, trail/pheromone behavior, recruitment, colony context and realistic development/population timing.

## Additional arthropods

1. **Liposcelis bostrychophila — adult domestic booklouse**
   - baseline tiny wingless crawler;
   - strain/form-aware physical scale;
   - direct behavioral/development measurements where available;
   - raw contextual study files do not become locomotion calibration unless they measured locomotion.

2. **Blattella germanica — ~24 h first instar**
   - exact-stage bounded-space locomotion model remains primary;
   - adult raw datasets/videos are valuable context/validation but do not override the first-instar model.

3. **Oryzaephilus surinamensis — adult sawtoothed grain beetle**
   - direct adult speed/move-stop-climb evidence;
   - raw/open physiology, cuticle, mortality and population datasets provide additional context;
   - current Strict Realistic remains crawling-only under the cited “no record of flight” evidence boundary, without calling the animal physiologically flightless.

4. **Chelifer cancroides — optional rare adult pseudoscorpion predator**
   - Arachnida/Pseudoscorpiones, not Insecta;
   - honest ~3 mm-class body plus 7–9 mm extended pedipalp visual envelope;
   - unusually strong direct adult locomotion/gait evidence;
   - predation remains evidence-classified: direct Varroa destructor + Liposcelis entomophila experiment; same-genus transfer to rendered L. bostrychophila; broader ant-prey evidence; no Linepithema humile-specific quantitative kill/pursuit/preference calibration.

## R8.3 evidence change

R8.2 was too image-heavy. R8.3 retains roughly half of the best visual-reference set and adds actual raw/open study data, source-data workbooks, experimental recordings, direct published numerical tables/model parameters, and clearly indexed remote-only high-value material where redistribution is not legal or clear.

Biological accuracy > byte count > visual novelty.
