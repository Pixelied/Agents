# R8.2 unresolved evidence gaps

These are intentional gates, not reasons to fabricate constants.

## Liposcelis bostrychophila
- clean untreated per-individual trajectories/speed distribution;
- turn-angle/path-persistence and pause/move-bout distributions;
- exact adult appendage envelope for the selected domestic form;
- display-like smooth-surface traversal;
- target-species disturbance/startle analog.

## Blattella germanica first instar
- exact-stage articulated footfall/gait dataset;
- exact first-instar neighbor-density arrest/aggregation curve extraction;
- target-stage cursor-like disturbance analog;
- corpse/smear material reference.

## Oryzaephilus surinamensis adult
- raw XY trajectories from direct movement studies;
- articulated adult footfall/gait evidence;
- display/glass traction/climbing calibration;
- direct startle/disturbance response;
- any future flight gate requires direct target-context evidence rather than assumption.

## Chelifer cancroides adult
- supplemental EthoVision tables/trajectories should be acquired if redistribution/access permits before claiming a complete natural roaming-speed distribution;
- smooth glass/display traction is not directly calibrated;
- attack/handling kinematics and encounter timing for the rendered prey set are incomplete;
- L. bostrychophila is not the exact Liposcelis prey species in the 2026 predation study;
- no Linepithema humile-specific predation rate/preference/pursuit distance;
- nymphal morphology/gait is not qualified for implementation as scaled adults;
- exact cursor-like disturbance analog remains absent.

## Large visual corpus boundary
The ~1 GB R8.1 corpus is valuable exact-species visual/reference material, but much of it is stage-unlabeled occurrence imagery. It is not a substitute for trajectory data, calibrated morphometry, or manual specimen re-identification. Use it for material/color/pose/silhouette validation unless a specific item is separately promoted with audited metadata.
