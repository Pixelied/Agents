# R8.2 Strict Realistic behavior gate

## Global rule
A biologically meaningful behavior enters **Strict Realistic** only when target-species/stage evidence, or an explicitly classified measured transfer, supports it. Engineering behavior may exist only when labeled as such.

## Argentine ant
Preserve the current evidence-backed Linepithema humile system, including worker variation, callows, queens, males, eggs/larvae/pupae/brood, recruitment and trail behavior. No fake soldier/major/minor castes and no walking “baby ant” brood.

## Liposcelis bostrychophila adult
Allowed: profile-specific 1:1 body scale, wingless crawl, slow hidden arrivals, realistic development timescale.
Gated: universal natural speed distribution, exact turn/pause distribution, target-species cursor response, exact glass/display traction, species-specific smear material.

## Blattella germanica ~24 h first instar
Allowed: exact-stage 3 x 2 mm body + 3 mm antenna scale, R3 bounded-space central/peripheral locomotion, stop mixture, wall/periphery behavior, hidden sibling arrivals.
Gated: exact-stage articulated footfall model, generic cursor fear, unextracted social-density curve, adult/different-instar locomotion transfers as direct data.

## Oryzaephilus surinamensis adult
Allowed: 1:1 flat adult scale, direct adult surface-speed anchors, untreated move/stop/climb timing, crack/crevice arrival narrative, crawling-only current Strict Realistic mode under the cited “no record of flight” source boundary.
Gated: claim of physiological flightlessness, raw-trajectory behaviors not actually acquired, articulated adult gait, generic cursor response, unmeasured glass adhesion.

## Chelifer cancroides adult — optional predator
Allowed: honest adult body + pedipalp scale, exact-species adult gait relationships, forward microstops, fast backward escape state, rare indoor arrivals.

Predation evidence classes must remain separate:
- direct target predator + direct prey species: Varroa destructor and Liposcelis entomophila;
- same-genus prey transfer: L. entomophila -> rendered L. bostrychophila;
- broader prey-category evidence: ants/booklice/small arthropods from extension sources;
- engineering: any Argentine-ant-specific kill rate, pursuit radius, handling time, preference ranking, or screen-derived prey sensing.

Strict Realistic must never make the predator home globally toward the nearest insect. Optional Ecology/Interactive mode may expose clearly labeled engineering predation parameters.

## Global blocks
- no universal cursor=predator mapping;
- no dark pixel=hideout/food/odor/host/prey mapping;
- no instant egg-to-adult growth;
- no cross-stage gait transfer labeled direct;
- no hidden visibility scale multiplier;
- no generated imagery as scientific authority;
- no GBIF reference photo used as numerical measurement authority unless separately audited as a calibrated specimen source.
