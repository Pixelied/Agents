## Current additional arthropods

Baseline crawlers:
1. adult Liposcelis bostrychophila;
2. ~24 h first-instar Blattella germanica;
3. adult Oryzaephilus surinamensis.

Optional rare ecology species:
4. adult **Chelifer cancroides** — house pseudoscorpion predator (Arachnida, not Insecta).

Chelifer should normally be absent or extremely sparse. Its body is ~3 mm-class but extended pedipalps create a much larger 7–9 mm visual envelope. Do not shrink it to match the baseline micro-crawlers.

## Predator evidence boundary

Chelifer predation is real, but do not fabricate target-specific rates:
- direct 2026 experiment: Varroa destructor + Liposcelis entomophila;
- transfer to our L. bostrychophila is same-genus evidence;
- extension evidence includes ants as a prey category;
- there is no current Linepithema humile-specific kill-rate/pursuit/preference dataset.

The current implementation plan tells you exactly which predator behaviors can enter Strict Realistic versus optional Ecology/Interactive mode.

It is **not** a 1 GB trajectory dataset and must not override audited physical measurements, exact-stage scientific images, locomotion papers or normalized empirical tables.
