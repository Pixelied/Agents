## Why this source matters
Comprehensive baseline for ant anatomy, sensory systems, communication, recruitment, trails, tactile interaction and colony-level behavior.

## Measurements / observations extracted
The work surveys ant anatomy and physiology together with chemical, tactile and visual communication, recruitment, trunk trails, learning and foraging. It is used as a broad map of which behaviors are biologically established and which primary papers to prioritize.
