## Why this source matters
Direct Linepithema humile pheromone-response model for individual turning behavior.

## Measurements / observations extracted
Ants turn in response to local pheromone concentration while speed is largely unaffected. Turning depends on left/right concentration contrast relative to total concentration, consistent with a Weber-like response; local information ahead of the ant predicts direction change.

## Units and uncertainty
Pheromone estimates are image-derived; geometry includes a roughly 1 cm sensing neighborhood in the reported analysis.

## What can enter the derived database
Trail-following steering response and separation of heading control from base speed.

## Conflicts / limitations
Do not treat the model as a universal ant trail rule; species/context specific.
