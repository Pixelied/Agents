## Why this source matters
Compact synthesis linking spatial motion, brief antennal encounters, density and collective state changes.

## Measurements / observations extracted
Ant interactions are often brief olfactory/antennal contacts. Encounter rate depends on local density and can itself regulate collective activity; movement changes can feed back on subsequent encounter rates.

## Units and uncertainty
