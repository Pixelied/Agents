## Why this source matters
Primary licensing and attribution source for a huge standardized ant specimen-image corpus.

## Measurements / observations extracted
AntWeb states its content is Creative Commons Attribution, requires photographer/specimen-code/source attribution for images, and warns that some photos/drawings have their own copyright statements.

## Units and uncertainty
No locomotion units; this is provenance/licensing evidence.

## What can enter the derived database
Permitted specimen metadata and image-provenance rules; candidate source for dorsal/profile/head reference images after item-level checks.

## What must not be redistributed
Only bundle individual assets whose own image page confirms a compatible license; preserve photographer, specimen code and source link.

## Conflicts / limitations
Site-level statement does not override item-level exceptions.
