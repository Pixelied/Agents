## Why this source matters
Excellent Linepithema humile locomotion source with thousands of limb videos.

## Measurements / observations extracted
Continually uneven terrain reduced preferred and peak walking speeds by up to 42%. Across more than 8000 limb-motion videos, speed modulation was driven more by stride-frequency change than stride-length change.

## Units and uncertainty
Walking speed and kinematic variables reported in the paper; terrain geometry controlled by 3D-printed substrates.

## What can enter the derived database
Argentine-ant speed/stride-frequency modulation and performance variability.
