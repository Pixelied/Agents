## Why this source matters
Independent evidence that slope changes locomotor/path-integration behavior and step geometry.

## Measurements / observations extracted
Desert ants walking on inclines alter the relationship between horizontal distance, surface distance and their stride-based odometry; the source directly connects slope, walking geometry and distance estimation.

## Units and uncertainty
Inclination angle and path/stride distances as reported.

## What can enter the derived database
Orientation/slope modifier evidence; useful for later non-flat surfaces, not a default monitor parameter.

## Conflicts / limitations
