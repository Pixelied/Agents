## Why this source matters
Tests whether gait coordination survives a major physical perturbation.

## Measurements / observations extracted
Loaded Cataglyphis fortis retains alternating tripod coordination but deforms spatial tripod geometry with center-of-mass shift; mean walking speed/stride metrics are reduced under load.

## Units and uncertainty
Load ratios 1.3–6.4 in the reported experiments; kinematics remain source-specific.

## What can enter the derived database
Robustness rule: preserve tripod phasing during burdened/disturbed walking while modifying spatial reach and speed.
