## Why this source matters
Primary smooth/vertical-surface biomechanics source—the monitor-face scenario we actually care about.

## Measurements / observations extracted
Ants use pretarsal adhesive pads plus tarsal hair arrays. On vertical surfaces upper legs can pull using adhesive pads while lower legs push using frictional contacts; experiments include smooth substrates and high-frequency force measurements.

## Units and uncertainty
Forces and timing as reported; worker mass and substrate conditions must remain source-linked.
