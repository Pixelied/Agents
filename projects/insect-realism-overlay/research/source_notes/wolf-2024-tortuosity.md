## Why this source matters
Modern trajectory-statistics source for realistic non-spline search/approach paths.

## Measurements / observations extracted
Directional changes measured across more than two orders of magnitude of path scale show Brownian/random-walk signatures even in approach trajectories, with context-dependent deviations near goals and with experience.

## Units and uncertainty
Turning angle versus arclength scale; trajectory geometry rather than a fixed turn rate.

## What can enter the derived database
Multiscale tortuosity targets for exploration/search and evidence against perfectly smooth game-agent paths.

## What must not be redistributed

## Conflicts / limitations
Desert-ant navigation trajectories are not equivalent to pheromone-trail household-ant paths.
