## Why this source matters
Independent pheromone/route-learning evidence showing that trail presence and memory are not interchangeable.

## Measurements / observations extracted
Pheromone presence influences route choice/deposition behavior but the experiments did not support a simple pheromone-as-learning-reassurance effect. Pheromone deposition is a stereotyped short pause/back-up/abdomen-contact action, reported around 0.2 s.

## Units and uncertainty
Behavioral decision and deposition timing; maze dimensions in centimeters.

## What can enter the derived database
Optional pheromone-deposition pause state and caution against making trails deterministic rails.

## Conflicts / limitations
