## Why this source matters
Experimental traffic source for group flow, headway and density behavior.

## Measurements / observations extracted
The study extracts flow-density, velocity and distance-headway distributions on a natural unidirectional trail. Average velocity is reported as nearly independent of density over the measured regime and no jammed phase appears.

## Units and uncertainty
Density, velocity, flow and distance headway as reported.

## What can enter the derived database
Group-lane/headway distribution targets and warning against car-traffic-style stop-and-go jams.
