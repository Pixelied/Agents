## Why this source matters
Independent morphology check for the 2–4 mm target envelope.

## Measurements / observations extracted
Worker total length is reported around 2.2–2.6 mm, with head length 0.70–0.76 mm, head width 0.61–0.69 mm and 12-segmented antennae without a club.

## Units and uncertainty
Millimeters.
