## Why this source matters
Best quantitative ant-specific source for active antenna motion and odor-trail sensorimotor coupling.

## Measurements / observations extracted
High-resolution video identifies probing, sinusoidal and trail-following modules. Left/right antenna movements are anti-correlated. During trail following, bilateral antenna position predicts body-angle change with a best-fit lag of 133 ms; video was acquired at 60 fps and analyzed at 30 fps.

## Units and uncertainty
Spatial resolution about 0.22 mm/pixel in reported analysis; timing ms; speed mm/s; normalized antenna positions.

## What can enter the derived database
Antenna motion states, anti-phase coordination, turn-lag evidence and stationary-probing threshold ideas.
