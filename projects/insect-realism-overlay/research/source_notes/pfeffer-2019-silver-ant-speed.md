## Why this source matters
High-speed gait/leg-motion source that gives hard upper-envelope kinematic measurements.

## Measurements / observations extracted
C. bombycina reaches stride frequencies up to about 47 Hz, stance phases down to 7 ms, leg swing speeds around 1.3–1.4 m/s and tripod coordination strength around 0.8; aerial phases appear at lower speeds than in C. fortis.

## Units and uncertainty
Walking/swing speeds mm/s; stride frequency Hz; phase duration ms; coordination dimensionless.

## What can enter the derived database
Upper-bound cadence/phase relationships and proof that gait dynamics change strongly with speed/body morphology.

## Conflicts / limitations
Extreme desert specialist; values are not defaults for 2–4 mm household ants.
