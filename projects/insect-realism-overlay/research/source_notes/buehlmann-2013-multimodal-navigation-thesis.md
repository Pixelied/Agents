Direct dissertation-level treatment of path integration, visual/olfactory navigation, systematic search and odor-plume following in Cataglyphis.

## Measurements / observations extracted
The 87-page PDF explicitly covers path integration, visually guided navigation, olfactory-guided navigation, systematic search, nest-plume following and olfactory foraging. It also notes Cataglyphis foragers can be extremely fast and normally forage individually rather than follow shared pheromone trails.
