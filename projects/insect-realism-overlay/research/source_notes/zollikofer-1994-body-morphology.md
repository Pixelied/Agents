## Why this source matters
Shows how stride geometry scales with ant leg morphology and body size.

## Measurements / observations extracted
Footfall geometry scales with leg dimensions. At high speed some workers exhibit intermittent aerial phases. Stride length follows s=a*v^b with b roughly 0.3–0.6 across species while factor a scales with leg length.

## Units and uncertainty
Stride length, speed and leg dimensions as reported per species.

## What can enter the derived database
Size-aware gait scaling and evidence that smaller ants should not simply use a resized large-ant animation.

## Conflicts / limitations
Scaling coefficients are species-specific; do not use a single universal exponent.
