## Why this source matters
Fire-ant climbing study with body-scale geometry, fast vertical motion and antenna/wall slip-recovery behavior.

## Measurements / observations extracted
Solenopsis invicta body length is reported as 3.5 ± 0.5 mm, directly overlapping our target scale. Ants move over 9 body lengths/s in tunnels and use limbs/body/antennae to arrest slips; critical arrest geometry scales with body length.

## Units and uncertainty
Body length mm; speed body-lengths/s; tunnel diameter normalized by body length.

## What can enter the derived database
