## Why this source matters
Open-access quantitative source connecting stride length, speed, stance and aerial phases.

## Measurements / observations extracted
Stride length increases with speed; stance duration shortens. At sufficiently high speed duty factor drops below 0.5 and aerial phases occur, supporting a walk-to-run transition rather than a single fixed animation cadence.

## Units and uncertainty
Speed in mm/s, temporal phases in seconds/milliseconds, stride length in millimeters.

## What can enter the derived database
Speed-dependent stride/duty-factor curves and transition criteria, with species transfer clearly marked.

## Conflicts / limitations
C. fortis is larger and much faster than the primary household target.
