## Why this source matters
Large annotated multi-ant trajectory source for interactions and clustering.

## Measurements / observations extracted
The paper reports 10 videos, 5,354 annotated frames, 712 ants and 114,112 ant annotations with identity/location information across indoor and outdoor scenes.

## Units and uncertainty
Frame coordinates/identities; conversion to physical millimeters requires scene calibration and cannot be guessed.

## What can enter the derived database
Interaction topology, path-shape and group-configuration statistics after calibration/quality checks.

## What must not be redistributed
Do not bundle dataset bytes until exact dataset license is verified.

## Conflicts / limitations
Physical scale/species may be absent or inconsistent across scenes.
