## Why this source matters
Open primary source for long-duration, identity-preserving tracking in densely interacting social insects.

## Measurements / observations extracted
anTraX represents tracking data as a graph and combines segmentation with neural-network identity classification, allowing individual tracks even through interactions/occlusions. The paper points to benchmark data on Zenodo.

## Units and uncertainty
Tracking accuracy/performance metrics are dataset-specific; spatial values require calibration.

## What can enter the derived database
Tracking-data provenance and architecture ideas for extracting real behavior distributions.

## What must not be redistributed
eLife article is CC BY. anTraX software itself has separate GPL licensing and is audited later.

## Conflicts / limitations
Color-tagged experimental animals differ visually from untagged household ants.
