## Why this source matters
Primary taxonomic/morphometric reference for the tiny Argentine ant, our strongest initial household-ant candidate.

## Measurements / observations extracted
Worker measurements include head length 0.62–0.78 mm, head width 0.53–0.72 mm, scape length 0.62–0.80 mm and other segment measurements across n=81 workers.

## Units and uncertainty
Linear measurements are millimeters as reported by the taxonomic work.

## Conflicts / limitations
