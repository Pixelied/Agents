## Why this source matters
Core ant gait source directly linking speed, turning and footfall geometry across 12 species.

## Measurements / observations extracted
Alternating tripod gait prevails over a wide speed range. Tripod geometry remains stable across speed/curvature. On curves, inside stride length shortens while outside stride length is largely independent of curvature.

## Units and uncertainty
Pages 95–106; stride/footfall values are species-specific and should be digitized/entered with their original units when needed.
