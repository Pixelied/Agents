Playback alignment is approximate and based only on the event anchors you provide. Large mismatches between anchors can indicate cuts, retiming, bad anchors, or unrelated video clocks—not necessarily deception.

Jolt can detect game-clock discontinuities, not intent. A green interval is not proof every intervening frame is original; a second POV near a cut is a lead for review, not reconstructed footage.
