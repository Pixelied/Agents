# Linepithema humile brood correction

The primary immature-stage study distinguishes worker and male larvae morphologically.

Worker larvae have a dorsal protuberance:
- L1 mean length 0.57 +/- 0.09 mm, mean width 0.29 +/- 0.05 mm, n=42; head width about 0.18 mm.
- L2 mean length 0.78 +/- 0.14 mm, mean width 0.40 +/- 0.08 mm, n=61; head width about 0.22 mm.
- L3 mean length 1.23 +/- 0.29 mm, mean width 0.59 +/- 0.12 mm, n=73; head width about 0.26 mm.

Male larvae lack that dorsal protuberance:
- L1 mean length 0.62 +/- 0.08 mm, n=11; head width about 0.18 mm.
- L2 mean length 1.18 +/- 0.26 mm, n=12; head width about 0.24 mm.
- L3 mean length 1.59 +/- 0.41 mm, n=10; head width about 0.26 mm.

Additional direct measurements:
- egg: 0.30 +/- 0.03 mm long, 0.23 +/- 0.01 mm wide, n=150;
- worker pupa: 2.41 +/- 0.09 mm long, n=60;
- male pupa: 3.18 +/- 0.12 mm long, n=30.

Implementation rule: brood is nest-context, non-adult locomotion. Worker and male larval series must remain separate. Queen brood remains quantitatively gated where direct measurements are absent.
