# Secondary-creature qualification

**Decision: ants only.**

Reference-only media are not redistributed. Scientific facts may inform independent implementation, but no supplied candidate passes the combined measurements, anatomy, behavior, reuse and ant-quality gate. A new or changed evidence base requires a fresh review.

All five source notes defer qualification pending independent size, locomotion and reuse evidence. Their original rows and license records are retained in the adjacent JSON.
