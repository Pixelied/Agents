# Data attribution and reuse

## Adapted runtime trajectory data

The runtime profile adapts a selected normalized portion of the dataset:

Valentini et al. (2020), *Revealing the structure of information flows
discriminates similar animal social behaviors*. eLife 9:e55395.
Article DOI: https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.55395
Dataset DOI: https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.9786260.v1
Dataset license recorded in the supplied Mega Pack: Creative Commons Attribution
4.0 International (CC BY 4.0): https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Changes: selected trajectory window and roles; normalization into physical
units; derived intervals and motion statistics; serialization into the app's
runtime profile. Original source identifiers and frames remain in provenance.
This is an adaptation, not an endorsement by the original authors, and not a
claim that the donor trajectory species/context is identical to the target.

## Other research

Other source notes inform numeric facts, species context and explicit model
assumptions. Reference-only articles, publisher PDFs, video frames, third-party
meshes and unaudited visual assets are not distributed with this application.
The renderer and utility icon are original procedural code. No source-code
reuse from incompatible licensed simulation projects is introduced here.

The source checkpoint does not duplicate the original Mega Pack. Keep that
separate supplied artifact for full evidence audits and raw-input rebuilds.
