## Why this source matters

Antenna positioning changes with behavioral state rather than following one universal motion.

## Measurements / observations extracted

Camponotus pennsylvanicus showed probing, sinusoidal and trail-following strategies. Analysis used 30 frames/s and 0.22 mm/pixel. The 13.2 mm/s probing cutoff is a classification rule, not a recommended walking speed.

## Units and uncertainty

The reported approximately 4 mm segment is head-center to body-centroid distance, not total body length.

## Conflicts / limitations

These carpenter-ant measurements do not directly define a 2-4 mm worker profile. No sweep frequency, amplitude mean or uncertainty is invented.
