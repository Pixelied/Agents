## Why this source matters

Argentine-ant gait provides a candidate species-matched anchor.

## Measurements / observations extracted

Linepithema humile speed variation was more closely associated with stride frequency than stride length. Reported flat-ground stride lengths primarily spanned 1.8-2.6 mm.

## Units and uncertainty

This is not a population minimum/maximum or a fitted default. No SD or model coefficient is reconstructed.

## Conflicts / limitations

Straight-stride selection limits turning inference. Horizontal uneven substrates do not establish smooth vertical-screen gait.
