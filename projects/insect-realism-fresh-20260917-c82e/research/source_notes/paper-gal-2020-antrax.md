## Why this source matters

## Measurements / observations extracted

The method combines neural classification with graph-based identity propagation. Exported assignment categories distinguish direct identification, inference and unresolved ambiguity.

## Units and uncertainty

Identity confidence is not physical calibration or a measured movement parameter.
