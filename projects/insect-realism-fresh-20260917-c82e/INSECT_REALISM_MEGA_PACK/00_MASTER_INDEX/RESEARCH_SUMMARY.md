## Page-credit rule at this checkpoint

A discovered citation, search excerpt, partial HTML reading, abstract, duplicated mirror, source-code file count, or dataset byte size does not automatically earn reviewed pages. All current reviews with unresolved completeness/figure/data checks are held at zero. Subsequent credit must record actual reviewed pages or conservative equivalent content units, their boundaries, exclusions and reviewer assessment. The acceptance threshold has not been reduced or bypassed.
